UKMTO receives report of incident 30 nautical miles southwest of Yemen's Hodeidah

DUBAI: A cargo ship came under attack Sunday off the coast of Yemen in the Red Sea, the British military said Sunday. The United Kingdom Maritime Trade Operations center reported the attack off Yemen, saying the ship reported being “under attack by unknown armed assailants.” The UKMTO said authorities were investigating. No group immediately claimed the attack. Yemen’s Houthi rebels have threatened to begin attacking ships again, though they have not carried out attacks. A Houthi spokesman did not immediately respond to a request for comment. Somali pirates have been active further away in the Gulf of Aden recently, as well.
